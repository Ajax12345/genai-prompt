Privacy Policy

COSI Student AI Usage — how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: August 25, 2026

1. Overview

COSI Student AI Usage ("the App") is an internal tool developed for the Brandeis University Computer Science Department. It allows students to disclose their use of AI tools when completing course assignments, and allows instructors to manage courses, assignments, and review submitted disclosures. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

2. Who We Are

The App is operated by the Brandeis University Computer Science Department. If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, contact James Petullo at jamespetullo@brandeis.edu.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information students provide directly

When submitting an AI usage disclosure, students provide their email address and, if applicable, the text of their AI conversation logs (prompts and model responses) for the relevant assignment. Students who did not use AI may indicate this instead of submitting a log. Submission is associated with the course and assignment it was made for.

3.2 Information from Google Sign-In (instructors)

Instructors sign in using their Brandeis Google Workspace account. When you sign in, we receive your name, email address, and Google account identifier from Google, limited to accounts on the brandeis.edu domain. We do not receive your Google password, and we do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, or other Google services beyond basic sign-in information.

3.3 Course and assignment data

Instructors may create courses and assignments within the App. This data (course names, codes, and assignment names) is stored to organize student disclosures and is visible to the instructor who created it.

3.4 Automatically collected information

The App uses session cookies solely to keep you signed in. We do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking. Submission timestamps are recorded automatically when a disclosure is submitted.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Operate the disclosure form and instructor dashboard as functioning tools;
  • Allow instructors to view disclosures submitted for their own courses and assignments;
  • Support academic integrity and departmental research into how students use AI tools in coursework;
  • Maintain the security and proper functioning of the App.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell your information.

6. How We Share Your Information

We do not share your information with third parties, except:

  • With the instructor(s) of the course a disclosure was submitted for;

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, including transmitting data over encrypted connections (HTTPS) in production, storing instructor passwords using a salted hashing algorithm where password sign-in is used, and restricting access to the underlying database to authorized personnel. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Choices and Rights

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, to request a correction, or to ask about deletion of your information, subject to any recordkeeping obligations under university policy or law (including FERPA). Students may decline to submit an AI conversation log and instead indicate that AI was not used, as provided for in the disclosure form itself.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use by Brandeis University students, instructors, and staff, and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
